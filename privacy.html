Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Welcome to FRL GAMES LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile games (including FR Legends 2) and use our related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a high-quality racing game experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (like IDFA for iOS or GAID for Android), IP address, and language settings.
  • Gameplay Data: Game progress, custom car configurations, lap times, achievements, and in-game purchase history.
  • Analytics: Automated telemetry regarding how you interact with our game loops, crashes, and performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly to maintain infrastructure and legally enhance security:

  • To synchronize game progression and account data across multiple devices.
  • To identify, patch, and prevent bugs, cheating, hacking, or unfair play styles.
  • To comply with localized tax regulations for legal in-app financial reporting.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with vetted technical third-party vendors, such as mobile platform providers (Apple App Store / Google Play Store) and analytics infrastructure partners. These networks process data securely under strict cryptographic non-disclosure mandates.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your region, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You can also restrict data processing or reset your mobile device advertising identifier via your iOS/Android native system settings to opt out of targeted analytical tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

Our mobile games are not intentionally marketed or targeted toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly harvest, log, or store personal profiles of children under strict COPPA protections. If you believe we hold such data accidentally, contact us immediately.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy requests or inquiries regarding your data, please contact our legal compliance representative via email at: support@frlgames.com
