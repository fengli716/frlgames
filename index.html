FR LEGENDS

FR LEGENDS was developed by a two-person team who are now the core developers behind FRL GAMES.

FR LEGENDS is all about drifting! From driving legendary FR (front-engine, rear-wheel-drive) drift cars at world’s most iconic circuits, to customize everything on your car including engine swaps and wide-body kits. Experience tandem drift battles alongside AI drivers with realistic scoring rules based on real-world competition judging.
